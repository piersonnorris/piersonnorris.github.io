What the numbers mean

The record grows from a small 2023 baseline into a more consistent 2024 and 2025 practice. “Recorded lifting volume” is the sum of logged weight multiplied by logged reps. It describes the training log; it is not a claim about unique pounds moved or a complete measure of athletic ability.
