# Athletics in Pierson's life

Athletics are part of Pierson Norris's life as a practice of consistency, effort, and self-direction. The training record shows a long-running habit of showing up: lifting, working through different muscle groups, and making room for movement alongside school, work, and building.

This public note is an aggregate snapshot of the logged Hevy workout records from 2023–2025. It intentionally excludes individual workout details. Running mileage is not included because the source record does not provide a reliable distance log.

Race records, event details, and results are kept in the private Obsidian athletics graph for later review. They are not part of this public snapshot.

## Reading the data

The record grows from a small 2023 baseline into a much more consistent 2024 and 2025 practice. The 2025 log contains the most workouts, sets, reps, recorded load, and exercise variety. “Lifted volume” is the sum of recorded weight multiplied by recorded reps; it is a training-log measure, not a claim about unique pounds moved or a direct measure of athletic ability.

## Sources and limits

- Window: October 19, 2023 through December 31, 2025; 2026 is excluded.
