Obsidian — general vault

Notes.

Everyday working notes: Markdown, tags, [[wikilinks]] and backlinks. Encrypted with your PIN and stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded, and nothing lands in the repo. Stock notes live in their own separate vault inside the tracker.

Export regularly. AES-256-GCM, key derived from your PIN with PBKDF2 at 600k iterations — which also means clearing site data or switching browsers loses the vault. Export writes one .md per note with YAML frontmatter; Import reads them back, or takes a whole .json bundle from tools/obsidian-sync.js.
