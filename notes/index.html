Obsidian — general vault

Notes.

Everyday working notes: Markdown, tags, [[wikilinks]] and backlinks. Encrypted with your PIN and stored in this browser only — nothing is uploaded, and nothing lands in the repo. Stock notes live in their own separate vault inside the tracker.

Round-trips with Obsidian

Export gives you a .zip of one .md per note, with YAML frontmatter — unzip it into your vault. Import reads .md files straight back in.

Linking works

Type [[Note title]] and it becomes a link in preview. Click a link to a note that doesn't exist yet and it gets created. Backlinks show at the bottom of every note.

Where it lives

AES-256-GCM, key derived from your PIN with PBKDF2 at 600k iterations. Clearing site data or switching browsers loses the vault — export regularly.
